Flagship project / Beta

Resona

An AI companion and support platform for children and families, designed around patience, personalization, and safer communication support.

01

What it is

Resona gives children a companion they can talk with, play with, and practice with. Parent tools provide oversight, profile context, safety settings, and support for communication needs.

02

Who it is for

Families who want AI support that understands a child over time, including children with speech differences, disability support needs, anxiety, or other communication gaps.

03

Why it matters

Many tools expect clear typing, clear speech, or adult-style conversation. Resona is built for children who may need more context, repetition, warmth, and flexibility.

04

Current phase

Resona is in beta. The current focus is reliability, family setup, voice support, safety, and high-quality companion responses before broader release.
